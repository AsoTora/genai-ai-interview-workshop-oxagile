## Brand Fonts

Company presentation and marketing materials
Headings and accent phrases — **Montserrat Semi-bold.**
For lead paragraphs and large copy — **Montserrat Light**
For plain-text  —**Segoe UI**

We use the following fonts on the site:

1) Headings and accent phrases — **Open Sans.**
2) For text  —  **Montserrat.**

We pay particular attention to the text hierarchy. Typography allows us to create clear, easy-to-read content with a clear structure.
